# InMail template

**Subject** (under 60 chars, specific): `Your <Team> <Role> post` · `<Background> interested in <Org>` · `Re: your <Role> post`

```
Hi <First>,

<HOOK: one sentence referencing something specific of theirs: a role they posted,
the org they recruit for, or a post. e.g. "I saw your post about the Senior Software
Engineer role on Azure Core, especially the focus on complex debugging.">

<PROOF: 2–3 facts from candidate-profile.md chosen by the angle map, with numbers.
e.g. "I'm a backend engineer at <Co> on <area>, where I <metric>. Before that I <metric>.">

<CREDENTIAL: one line, verified only. e.g. "I'm also top X% in competitive programming
and teach system design on YouTube and LinkedIn.">

<ASK, one only:
  🟢 "Would you be open to me sending my resume for that role, or anything similar on <team>?"
  🟡/🔴 "Could I send my resume, or would you point me to the recruiter who covers <area>?">

Best,
```
(Sales Navigator appends the signature, so stop at "Best,".)

## Checklist before staging
- [ ] Every claim appears in candidate-profile.md
- [ ] Hook is specific to this recruiter (not reusable for anyone else)
- [ ] No mutual-connection names unless the user knows them
- [ ] No location or relocation claim unless stated by the user
- [ ] 80–130 words
