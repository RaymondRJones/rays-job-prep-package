# Recruiter message template

Rules and reasoning live in `skills/inmail-drafts/SKILL.md`. This is the fill-in skeleton.

**Subject** (< 60 chars): `Re: your <Role> post` · `<Background> engineer for <Team>`

```
Hi <First>,

<HOOK: one sentence only this recruiter could receive: a role they posted, the team they
hire for, or a post of theirs.>

<PROOF: two plain-language results, one per job, matched to their desk. One number each,
no tool names. "I'm a <metro> <role> at <Co>, where I <outcome>. Before that I <outcome>.">

<SIGNAL: the strongest verified, user-approved "ready now / passed a similar bar" line.>

<ASK: one request.
  🟢 "Would you be open to me sending my resume for that role, or anything similar on <team>?"
  🟡/🔴 "Could I send my resume, or would you point me to the recruiter who covers <area>?">

Best,
```

- **InMail:** stop at `Best,` (Sales Navigator adds the signature).
- **Email:** add the full name and LinkedIn URL under `Best,`, plus "Resume attached" if the user attaches it.

## Checklist
- [ ] 80–130 words (aim ~100), subject < 60 chars
- [ ] Every claim and number is in candidate-profile.md
- [ ] A non-engineer understands every sentence (no tool names or jargon); 1–2 req keywords kept
- [ ] Hook is specific to this recruiter
- [ ] No mutual names, location, level, or offer claims unless the user approved them
